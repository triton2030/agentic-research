# Red Flags

- Do not launch first and explain later.
- Do not let two workers share ownership of the same files.
- Do not delegate the main agent's immediate blocking next step.
- Do not ask clarifying questions that would not change the split or the brief.
- Do not write broad briefs that invite drift.
- Do not replace a clear role card and success criteria with a long procedural checklist unless the sequence is truly load-bearing.
- Do not force a "launch or not?" confirmation pause unless the user explicitly asked for plan-first review.
- Do not turn an auditor into a shadow owner that re-decides strategy, architecture, and task criteria.
- Do not ask an auditor for final sign-off when there is no artifact-level evidence to inspect.
- Do not paste every launch brief into chat by default; keep them internal unless the user asked to inspect them.
- Do not quietly switch to some other execution flow while framing this as native subagent preparation.
- Do not treat clean `Strategy Compliance` in `_state/` as if the work were already done when `Distance to North Star` is still `in progress`.
- Do not ignore `_state/` when it names the remaining gap that should drive which workstream gets delegated now.
