# Chat recall

## Unique context

Treat the corpus as dated owner speech, never as current truth, a complete
history, or a derived profile; a precise quote may already be stale.

Capture buys future Retrieval. Retrieval resolves source, scope, chronology,
supersession, and live-owner evidence into an applicable position or an honest
`abstain`.

Never send corpus records to network tools.

## Choose the current mode

1. A new owner statement may change a future decision, boundary, criterion,
   preference, or understanding → fully read [Capture](references/capture.md).
2. Consequential work or a later question may depend on prior owner words →
   fully read [Retrieval](references/retrieval.md).
3. Retrieval ended `recovery-needed` because its normal route could not support
   a decision → fully read
   [Recovering recall coverage](references/reading-the-log.md).
4. This session is explicitly transferring recovered meanings into existing
   owner files → finish Retrieval first, then fully read
   [Restoring meanings](references/restoring-meanings.md).
5. A record is damaged, corpus structure needs explicit validation, or the
   owner requests pre-capture backfill → fully read
   [Repairing the log](references/repairing-the-log.md).

Complete triggered modes in numbered order, returning here after each; only the
reference-defined receipt closes a mode, never a candidate list or unread
address.
