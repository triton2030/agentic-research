# RPT — Research and Evidence Report

**Purpose:** answer a bounded research question with traceable findings,
uncertainty, contradictions and limitations. **Default authority:** `evidence`.
Alias: EVD. Near-miss: planned method → RSP; intervention test → EXP; market
requirements → MRD; consequential choice → DEC.

**Ban:** RPT owns evidence, not decisions. No recommendation is promoted to an
accepted choice; observation, calculation and inference stay distinguishable.

**Non-obvious contracts:** Local Implications = LOCAL — downstream owner links →
evidence-bounded possible effect, not a decision. Downstream Decisions and
Owners = REFERENCE. The negative space is owned, not optional: Contradictory and
Negative Evidence, What Is Not Proven, Open Hypotheses, and Confidence stated
per major claim with its reason.

**Conditional modules:** literature synthesis; quantitative appendix;
qualitative coding; benchmark; competitive review; legal-source hierarchy.

**Completion check:** denominator, as-of and source ledger are visible behind
every claim.
