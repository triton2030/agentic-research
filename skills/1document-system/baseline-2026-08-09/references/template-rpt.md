# RPT — Research and Evidence Report

**Purpose:** ответить на bounded research question с traceable findings,
uncertainty, contradictions и limitations. **Default authority:** `evidence`.
Alias: EVD. Near-miss: planned method → RSP; intervention test → EXP; market
requirements → MRD; consequential choice → DEC.

## Core Sections

| Heading | Mode | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question and Report Scope | OWNER | Exact question, protocol, as-of boundary |
| Evidence Window | OWNER | Collection/source dates and freshness |
| Method and Deviations | OWNER | Actual method and effects on inference |
| Source or Sample Ledger | OWNER | Included/excluded units and denominator |
| Evidence Quality and Limitations | OWNER | Bias, missingness, conflicts, generalizability |
| Findings | OWNER | Claim IDs, evidence, scope, fact/derived distinction |
| Contradictory and Negative Evidence | OWNER | Counter-signals and unresolved conflict |
| Comparisons and Calculations | OWNER | Methods, units, ranges, assumptions |
| Interpretation | OWNER | Meaning inside supported boundary |
| Confidence | OWNER | Per major claim, with reason |
| Local Implications | LOCAL | Downstream owner links → evidence-bounded possible effect, not decision |
| What Is Not Proven | OWNER | Tempting unsupported conclusions |
| Open Hypotheses | OWNER | Next uncertainties, not facts |
| Downstream Decisions and Owners | REFERENCE | DEC/MRD/PRD consumers |
| Reproducibility Material | OWNER | Data/calculation locations and access limits |

## Conditional Modules

Literature synthesis; quantitative appendix; qualitative coding; benchmark;
competitive review; legal-source hierarchy.

## Completion Check

Claims trace to sources/sample; observation/calculation/inference differ;
denominator, as-of, confidence, contradictions и limitations visible; no
recommendation is promoted to accepted decision.
