# MRD — Market Requirements Document

**Purpose:** превратить bounded market scope в current target-market model и
traceable requirements. **Default authority:** `canon`; raw studies остаются
`evidence` в RPT. Near-miss: evidence-only study → RPT; product choice → DEC;
feature behavior → PRD.

## Core Sections

| Heading | Mode | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Question, Scope, and As-of | OWNER | Category, geography, time boundary |
| Evidence Basis | REFERENCE | Reports/source ledger и freshness limits |
| Market Problem | OWNER | Recurring unmet outcome, actors, observed cost |
| Market Structure | OWNER | Actors, value chain, buying context, constraints |
| Segments | OWNER | Discriminating dimensions and definitions |
| Target ICP | OWNER | Selected segment, qualification signals, decision link |
| Jobs and Demand Situations | OWNER | Jobs, triggers, frequency, urgency |
| Pains and Desired Outcomes | OWNER | Ranked outcomes and evidence strength |
| Alternatives and Substitutes | OWNER | Current behavior, competitors, switching costs |
| Buying and Adoption Barriers | OWNER | Economic, trust, workflow, procurement barriers |
| Anti-segments | OWNER | Who not to target and why |
| Market Accessibility | OWNER | Market-scope reachability, channel constraints, evidence/calculation links |
| Market Requirements | OWNER | Stable IDs, actor, need, priority, evidence trace |
| Assumptions and Unresolved Questions | OWNER | Status and evidence needed |
| Exclusions and Handoff | OWNER | MRD-owned non-decisions, scope boundary, exact PRD/DEC handoff links |

## Conditional Modules

Geography/regulation; enterprise procurement; channel structure; seasonality;
price sensitivity; market sizing with explicit assumptions.

## Completion Check

Scope/as-of один; segments distinguishable; каждый requirement связан с need и
evidence; assumptions не выданы за facts; UI и implementation отсутствуют.
