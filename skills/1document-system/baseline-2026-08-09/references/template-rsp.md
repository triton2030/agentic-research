# RSP — Research Protocol

**Purpose:** precommit defensible method for one bounded question before new
evidence is collected/interpreted. **Default authority:** `ops`. Near-miss:
causal product test → EXP; completed findings → RPT; market synthesis → MRD.

## Core Sections

| Heading | Mode | Contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Decision Context | REFERENCE | Downstream question/DEC/PRD needing evidence |
| Research Questions | OWNER | Primary and subordinate answerable questions |
| Scope and Non-goals | OWNER | Population, context, time, unsupported claims |
| Existing Evidence | REFERENCE | Prior RPTs and gaps |
| Method and Rationale | OWNER | Design and question-method mapping |
| Population and Sample | OWNER | Unit, inclusion/exclusion, target, diversity |
| Recruitment and Selection | OWNER | Channels, screening, bias controls |
| Instruments and Collection | OWNER | Tasks/questions/measures, order, capture |
| Measures and Coding | OWNER | Variables and operational definitions |
| Analysis Plan | OWNER | Comparison, synthesis, missing-data treatment |
| Evidence Quality and Bias Controls | OWNER | Confounders, triangulation, researcher effects |
| Ethics, Privacy, and Consent | LOCAL | Ethics/privacy owner links → study-specific safeguards and consent delta |
| Stopping and Insufficient-evidence Rules | OWNER | Saturation/sample/quality boundaries |
| Interpretation Boundary | OWNER | Claims design can and cannot support |
| Execution Roles and Window | OWNER | Accountable researcher, dates, handoffs |
| Deviation Policy | OWNER | What may change and how recorded |
| Outputs and Canon Handoff | OWNER | RPT/data outputs and decision recipient |

## Conditional Modules

Preregistration; power calculation; incentives; vulnerable participants;
localization; data-retention plan.

## Completion Check

Each question maps to method/data/analysis; sample, stop, bias, ethics и
deviations explicit; unsupported conclusions named; findings not predetermined.
