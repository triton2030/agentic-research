# Projections

`projections/` is the free reader-facing zone: controlled canon duplicates,
HTML, diagrams, decks, any convenient format. The folder says «view»; lineage
proves which truth the view came from.

## The single hard boundary

Every projection carries traceable `derived-from` including relevant current
canon or durable evidence; a current claim never rests on ops alone. Markdown
minimum: `description` (reader job + scope), `authority: projection`,
`derived-from`, and `depends-on` for every source section whose change can
falsify a maintained claim. A dated snapshot may drop update edges only with
an explicit `as-of` boundary, excluded from current retrieval. Non-Markdown
formats register lineage in one companion or existing manifest — never a
manifest system for one artifact.

A projection may reorder, emphasize and explain. It may not add mutable truth,
promote hypothesis to fact, hide a material contradiction, or redefine an
owner. After update, check claims against sources: a conflict blocks
readiness; an unsupported claim gets `not-evidenced` or dies. A changed hard
source makes the projection stale until rechecked — folders and fresh dates
prove nothing.

Never use a projection as a source for canon, decision or evidence. Default
retrieval and refactor inventory ignore projection content.
