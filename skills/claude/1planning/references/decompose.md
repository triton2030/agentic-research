# Cutting work

Absorbed from `1break-down` (retired 2026-08-08): "cutting work is a core
function of planning and must live inside planning".

## Name the axis before the form

Name the cut axis and, in one line, why it is material: a dependent chain ·
independent branches · research with a decision gate · probing hard limits
· another natural shape.

Without a named axis the cut collapses into chronology — waterfall
("design → implement → verify") is always the most available narrative: a
tidy tree with the seams hidden. Anti-example: five non-overlapping items
by stage of work; the axis is unnamed — and nobody sees that the risk sat
in the dependency between item two and item four.

## Only to the nearest checkable frontier

Do not draw the whole tree in advance. Cut deeper where risk, a dependency
or an unknown blocks an honest next move. Further depth waits for new
evidence.

For every material block — input, expected output, check. Add no ceremony
to obvious steps.

## Unknown ≠ premise

An **unknown** is not yet taken as true — keep it a research step or a
decision gate on the path. A **premise** already holds the outcome, the
scope, or the path itself, being assumed true. Do not let an unknown become
a silent premise; a material premise stops the cut: one derivable from the
goal, the principles or owner records is resolved via `1use-principles`;
one requiring the owner's word — a blocking question per
[questions](questions.md). Once resolved: continue · cut the revealed
pre-phase · return the move to the owner of the approach, not of the
decomposition.

If the object of the work itself is ambiguous and different readings give
different frontiers — one owner question and stop; do not pick a reading by
the repo's theme.

## The epic map comes before the cut

A cut across several epics is a signal that the map is changing, not the
plan ([map](map.md)). One legitimate outcome of a map change: large
cross-cutting work becomes its own epic with its own home and `зависит-от`
edges. Subtasks do not stand in for epics; their depth within one epic
belongs to [contract](contract.md).

## Boundaries

- The cut is made once before heavy work, not at every substep.
- An obviously single step, plain reading, work already stably cut — skip.
- Cutting does not choose the approach and does not rebuild the goal: an
  unresolved main fork does not belong here.
