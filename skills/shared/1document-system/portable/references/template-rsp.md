# RSP — Research Protocol

**Purpose:** precommit a defensible method for one bounded question before new
evidence is collected or interpreted. **Default authority:** `ops`. Near-miss:
causal product test → EXP; completed findings → RPT; market synthesis → MRD.

**Ban:** RSP owns the method before the data, not the findings. No results, no
conclusions; a predetermined finding invalidates the protocol.

**Non-obvious contracts:** Decision Context and Existing Evidence = REFERENCE.
Ethics, Privacy and Consent = LOCAL: owner links → study-specific safeguard and
consent delta. Interpretation Boundary is owned: the claims this design can and
cannot support. Deviation Policy is owned: what may change and how it is
recorded. Stopping and Insufficient-evidence Rules are fixed in advance.

**Conditional modules:** preregistration; power calculation; incentives;
vulnerable participants; localization; data-retention plan.

**Completion check:** each question maps to a method, a data source and an
analysis step.
