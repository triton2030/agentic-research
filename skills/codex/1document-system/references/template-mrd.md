# MRD — Market Requirements Document

**Purpose:** turn a bounded market scope into a current target-market model and
traceable requirements. **Default authority:** `canon`; raw studies stay
`evidence` in RPT. Near-miss: evidence-only study → RPT; product choice → DEC;
feature behavior → PRD.

**Ban:** MRD owns the market need, not the product answer. No UI, no
implementation, no feature behavior; assumptions are never stated as facts.

**Non-obvious contracts:** Evidence Basis = REFERENCE — source ledger and
freshness limits, raw studies remain in RPT. Market Requirements carry stable
IDs with actor, need, priority and evidence trace. Anti-segments are owned: who
not to target and why. Exclusions and Handoff owns the MRD's non-decisions plus
exact PRD/DEC handoff links.

**Conditional modules:** geography/regulation; enterprise procurement; channel
structure; seasonality; price sensitivity; market sizing with explicit
assumptions.

**Completion check:** one scope and one as-of; every requirement traces to a
need and to evidence.
