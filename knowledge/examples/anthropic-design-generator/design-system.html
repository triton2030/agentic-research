Design System v1

The quiet
civic vocabulary.

One brand pair (deep civic blue + warm cream), four editorial tones, a serif-and-sans pairing, and a handful of patterns that compose into the whole product. Tokens are the canon; every screen is a rearrangement of the same parts.

Foundations

Semantic surfaces before pigments. Every token below maps to a role — background, foreground, accent, muted — not a hex. Swap the hex, keep the role, keep the system.

Semantic palette

Four tones

The bento rhythm uses four editorial surface tones. They rotate across cards the way a magazine rotates art direction — never two adjacent cards in the same tone.

Cream

Soft document surface. Most common.

Dusty

Cool civic register. Role-and-process moments.

Gold

Warm accent. Marks the weight of a number.

Sage

Grounded, trust-forward. Anchors proof.

Why start with smaller municipalities?

Because smaller towns carry meaningful contractor spend with lean teams and strong accountability pressure.

Is Civic-Chain primarily a blockchain company?

No. It's a municipal contracting and accountability platform first.

Is public input required for every contract?

No. It's a layer that supports the broader contract process where appropriate.
