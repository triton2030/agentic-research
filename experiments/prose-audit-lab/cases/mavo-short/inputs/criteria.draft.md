# Criteria Draft

Source: inferred from target project GOAL and root AGENTS. Not owner-approved.

1. Preserve the current core: paid `Принять`, immutable order snapshot, buyer
   pays studio, studio is seller/executor, MAVO owns catalog/SKU.
2. Ready-for-development traceability: decisions lead to owner files and facts
   or marked hypotheses.
3. Keep the first product simple enough for one founder, one studio, one order.
4. Make the business model explicit enough to identify go/no-go and kill
   conditions.
5. Defer scale-only marketplace/fraud/analytics complexity unless the current
   chain requires it.

Conflict rule: the lower-numbered criterion wins unless the audit exposes a
direct contradiction with business viability or a core claim.
