# MAVO can earn money under the current model

## Archived Test Metadata

- id: PROFIT-01
- class: chain
- oracle: 2-coherence
- runs: 3
- checks: traceability, warrant, rebuttal, reality-gap

## Question

Does the corpus contain a traceable chain showing how MAVO earns money under the
current SaaS-vitrine model, without smuggling in future marketplace or commission
logic?

## Required Chain

1. Current revenue source: who pays MAVO and for what.
2. Amount / pricing corridor: subscription, per-request fee, setup, or other
   current mechanism, with status as fact / hypothesis / assumption.
3. Cost structure: expected costs needed to serve one studio and one order.
4. Unit or period economics: how revenue exceeds direct costs, or what is still
   unknown.
5. Sensitivity / kill conditions: which assumptions break the model.
6. Reality check: cheapest experiment that can validate the weakest assumptions.

## Passing Standard

Each chain link has:

- claim;
- data / anchor: file and line or section;
- warrant: why the data supports the claim;
- qualifier: fact / hypothesis / assumption;
- rebuttal or defeater: when the claim stops holding.

Green means the documentation is internally traceable. It does not mean the
business is proven.
