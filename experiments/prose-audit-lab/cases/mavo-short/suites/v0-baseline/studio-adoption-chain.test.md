# Studios have a reason to connect and keep using MAVO

## Archived Test Metadata

- id: STUDIO-01
- class: chain
- oracle: 2-3-coherence-simulation
- runs: 3
- checks: traceability, warrant, rebuttal, reality-gap

## Question

Does the corpus contain a traceable chain showing why a studio would connect to
MAVO, safely onboard, fulfill first orders, and continue after month three?

## Required Chain

For the studio path from discovery to repeated use:

1. Studio pain or business job.
2. MAVO value against that pain.
3. Current alternative and why it is worse or insufficient.
4. Switching cost / onboarding friction and how the product reduces it.
5. Retention mechanism after first orders.
6. Risk handling: quality remains with studio, buyer pays studio, MAVO does not
   police production like a marketplace.
7. Reality check: interviews, pilot, or usage signal that can validate adoption.

## Passing Standard

Each chain link has claim, data / anchor, warrant, qualifier, and rebuttal.
The chain must respect the current model and keep future marketplace mechanics
out of the current verdict unless explicitly anchored as current.
