# Buyers have a reason to submit and pay for orders

## Archived Test Metadata

- id: CUSTOMER-01
- class: chain
- oracle: 2-3-coherence-simulation
- runs: 3
- checks: traceability, warrant, rebuttal, reality-gap

## Question

Does the corpus contain a traceable chain showing why a buyer would use the
studio's MAVO vitrine and reach the paid `Принять` boundary?

## Required Chain

For the buyer path from first visit to paid accepted order:

1. Buyer pain or job-to-be-done.
2. MAVO promise to the buyer.
3. Mechanism in the product surface that makes the promise concrete.
4. Trust / risk handling around payment, file readiness, production, pickup, and
   returns under the studio-as-seller boundary.
5. Why the buyer does not simply use the old offline/chat flow.
6. Reality check: how the demand assumption is validated after launch.

## Passing Standard

Each chain link has claim, data / anchor, warrant, qualifier, and rebuttal.
Mechanism must be falsifiable enough that a designer or developer could violate
it.
