# Audience Decision Duel

## Test Metadata

- id: MAVO-AUDIENCE-04
- class: decision-duel
- oracle: defender-challenger-judge
- required_roles: defender, challenger, judge

## Question

Is "start with small studios" better supported than an alternative wedge such
as mature W2P/MIS-capable studios, larger studios, or another segment visible in
the corpus?

## Options

- Option A: small / micro studios first.
- Option B: mature W2P/MIS-capable or larger studios first.
- Option C: another segment if the corpus supports it better.

## Criteria

1. Pain intensity.
2. Willingness to pay.
3. Activation friction.
4. Existing alternatives and substitutes.
5. Ability to bring buyer traffic.
6. Retention likelihood.
7. Cheapest primary validation.

## Passing Standard

The judge must say whether the corpus supports the chosen audience decision, or
whether the decision remains an owner hypothesis requiring primary evidence.
