# Typed evidence probe Wiki

This is one offline diagnostic transformation of the two typed-evidence clusters using the official OpenViking LLM Wiki page model. It is a derived projection for a later blind-reader check, not a stock OpenViking Compile result.

## Pages

- [Retrieval aid is not proof](<concept/Retrieval aid is not proof.md>) — why search cards and hits select a holder but do not prove the owner's position.
- [OpenViking chat-recall knowledge-library outcome](<concept/OpenViking chat-recall knowledge-library outcome.md>) — the five current outcome obligations, their chronology and their boundaries.

## Scope and limits

The deterministic builder owns exact membership, count, first/latest timestamps, SHA-256 and Git provenance. This offline page set preserves those facts and exact owner records, but it does not prove stock runtime availability, Compile success, retrieval quality or blind-reader acceptance. Claims outside the two supplied clusters remain unsupported.
